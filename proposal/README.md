# Proposal pack: taking Borrower Truth through a bank's approval process

Banks don't adopt a tool because it works. A sponsor carries it, control partners sign it off, and a committee approves a small, safe first step. This pack is built for that path.

| Stage | What happens | Document |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Socialize | Pitch informally to peers and a manager, find the roadblocks, secure a sponsor | the stakeholder map below; the deck (`deck/plan-deck.html`) |
| 2. Draft | A one-page memo for the sponsor; a business case (BRD) for the working team | `01-EXEC-MEMO.html` (one page); `02-BUSINESS-CASE.md` |
| 3. Control framework | Model risk, compliance (BSA/AML), legal, InfoSec, third-party risk, operational risk, audit | `03-CONTROL-MATRIX.md` |
| 4. Committee | Present with the sponsor; defend the metrics, alternatives and failure scenarios; approve a pilot, not a rollout | `05-COMMITTEE-DEFENSE.md`; `04-PILOT-PLAN.md` |

## Who needs to say yes (by role, not by name)
- **Executive sponsor:** the head of the business that lends to non-bank financial firms, or the senior credit officer who owns that portfolio's risk. They own the loss, so they own the case.
- **Business owner:** the NDFI credit officers and portfolio managers who would work the queue.
- **Control partners:**
  - Model Risk Management
  - Compliance, the financial-crimes / BSA-AML unit in particular
  - Legal
  - Information Security / Technology Risk
  - Third-Party Risk
  - Operational Risk
  - Internal Audit
- **Delivery:** the Commercial Banking technology team that would host it.

## How an intern would socialize it
1. Show the deck and the live demo to my manager and team first. Ask what would stop it, not whether they like it.
2. Ask my manager who owns NDFI credit risk, and request 20 minutes with someone on that team. Bring the one-page memo, not the deck.
3. Ask one Model Risk and one Compliance contact the questions in `03-CONTROL-MATRIX.md` early. Their objections shape the pilot, and it's cheaper to learn them before the committee.
4. Only then take a sponsor-backed memo to a steering committee, asking for a 90-day shadow pilot.

**Honest status:** everything here is prepared from public information and a prototype on synthetic data. It has not been seen by anyone at Wells Fargo.

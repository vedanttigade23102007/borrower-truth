# 90-day pilot plan (shadow mode)

**Ask:** a read-only pilot on one NDFI sub-portfolio. The tool never acts; its cases are reviewed alongside business as usual.

**Scope**
- About 20–40 borrowers: auto-finance warehouse facilities. These are where the real-data calibration is strongest (SEC ABS-EE covers auto loans).
- **Data (read-only):** monthly tapes, servicer-system access for pulls, collection- and operating-account activity, UCC and court records, disclosures, financial packages, PledgeCheck results.

**How it runs**
- **Weekly:** run the checks, then the credit officers review every "likely fraud" and a sample of "worth a look" cases.
- Each case gets a label: useful / not useful / already known, and the time it took to review.
- **Monthly:** thresholds re-checked on the bank's own tapes. The honest range comes from real books, as it did from the 28 public pools.

**Success criteria (set before starting)**
1. At least 50% of "likely fraud" cases are judged worth an officer's time.
2. Review load stays within what the team can absorb, e.g. under 5 officer-hours a week. The team sets this number up front.
3. No data incident. 100% of decisions replay identically.
4. At least one finding the existing process had not surfaced, or a clear statement that none appeared (itself a useful result).

**Kill criteria**
- Officers judge most cases not useful after threshold tuning, **or** the review load can't be brought under the agreed ceiling.
- Required data can't be obtained under the credit agreements.

**Governance**
- **Weekly:** stand-up with the business owner.
- **Monthly:** checkpoint with the sponsor.
- **Model risk:** involved from week 0.
- **Exit:** a go / no-go paper to the steering committee at the end of the pilot.

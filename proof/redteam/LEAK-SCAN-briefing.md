## Judgement of each hit (Claude, 1 Oct 2026; for Vedant to confirm before upload)

**No leak.** Every hit is innocent:

- **`deferment_program` (TABLES.md and `disclosures.kind`) and `judgment` (`court_records.kind`)** are values in the data the bank receives. A borrower sees these words in its own reporting anyway. They share a spelling with internal names (the generator's look-alike `DEFERMENT_PROGRAM` and the clue type `JUDGMENT`), but they say nothing about how anything is checked.
- **The numbers 2, 3, 30 and 60** are:
  - the late-payment buckets (30–59 and 60–89 days)
  - the quarter timing ("month 3q + 2")
  - the SEC item numbers (2.03, 2.04)
  - "2 borrowers per book"
  - a dollar figure inside the DOJ text ($2.2 billion)

  None of them is a threshold the checks use in that sense.

**Read by a person, beyond the scan:**
- **The three allegation texts** are the DOJ and SEC press releases, with the header our research script adds (source URL, date fetched). They don't mention the bank, this project or any code.
- **The sample book has no labels, notes or generator manifest.** The 6 borrowers with planted frauds were stripped out, along with the companies, people, liens and judgments that only they touched. Their IDs leave gaps in the numbering (B0018, B0019, B0029, B0030, B0034, B0037). That reveals only that some borrowers were removed, which README.md says openly.

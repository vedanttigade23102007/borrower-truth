Catch borrower fraud in loans to non-bank lenders months before a quarterly audit: a second pair of eyes next to covenants and field exams.

Decision requested: a read-only pilot on 20–40 auto-finance warehouse borrowers. About 19 FTE-months (~$0.5–1.0M planning range). No automated actions, no customer-facing change. Go / no-go returns to committee in 90 days.

Why now

  • Banks have lent $1.32T to non-bank lenders, 10% of all bank loans; banks over $100B hold about 86% (FDIC, Feb 2026). In the worked example it is Wells Fargo's largest commercial category: $208.8B, 20% of its loans (10-Q, June 2026), and Q1 2026 charge-offs rose "driven by a single fraud-related loss" (10-Q).
  • The 2025–26 frauds in this market (Tricolor, First Brands; SEC and DOJ allegations) used several tricks at once, hidden in different places; field exams and collateral checks each look in one place. After Q1, management said teams reviewed clients' procedures and collateral (earnings call); this makes that review monthly.
  • Model-risk guidance changed on 17 Apr 2026: SR 26-2 / OCC 2026-13 replaced SR 11-7 and OCC 2011-12. A new tool should bring a model-risk file written against it. Here, written rules decide; the AI agent only drafts.

What it is

Seven checks, one per documented trick, joined into one case per borrower: what's wrong, the evidence chain, a suggested fix and a drafted letter. An officer approves every step. Verdicts come from written rules, not a model; every decision can be replayed. It uses Setpoint PledgeCheck results (simulated here). Its models are open-weight and run inside the bank.

Money

Break-even, not a forecast: moving the response to one ~$8M exposure three months earlier pays for the pilot at 3% monthly erosion; the bank's own loss data replaces these inputs.

Risk and controls

  • Model risk: likely in scope as a model under SR 26-2. Register and tier it, and validate it independently before production. The AI agent is outside that guidance's scope and never decides.
  • BSA/AML: likely-fraud cases route to the financial-crimes unit for a SAR decision. Compliance and Legal approve the letter templates, so no letter tips off a borrower.
  • InfoSec and third parties: runs inside the bank, with bank SSO and encryption at rest (to build). Lien and court data vendors go through third-party review.

Next steps if approved
